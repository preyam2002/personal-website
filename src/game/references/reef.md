## Changes made because of these references

### Manta (`src/game/creatures/manta.ts`)
- **Shape:** a new planform (`mantaLead` / `mantaTrail`) gives a concave trailing edge and falcate, swept-back wingtips. The old trailing edge was nearly straight. A small dorsal fin sits at the tail base, with no caudal lump (reef manta).
- **Skin:** a baked 2048 px atlas (albedo, normal, ORM), folded at the trailing edge so the dorsal and ventral halves meet without a seam.
  - Dorsal side: near-black with mottling. Grey-white shoulder chevrons start behind the spiracles and fade to grey toward the back, curving medially and leaving the black "T" over the head. Spiracle slits and a few pale scars.
  - Ventral side: white belly with a dark grey trailing-edge band and grey wingtips. A dark mouth slot, five pairs of gill slits (grooves in the normal and AO maps), and dark identification spots: dense between the gill slits, sparse on the belly and wings.
  - Fine denticle relief; roughness about 0.35–0.45 for a wet mucus sheen.
- **Cephalic fins:** each is built twice, rolled into a spiral horn and unrolled into a cupped paddle, and the vertex shader blends between the two (`uCephalic`). The fins unroll while feeding (during the somersaults) and roll up again for cruising.
- **Timing:** the stroke period is now 2.8–3.8 s around the measured 0.31 Hz (it was 3–6 s), and 2.6 s while feeding. A phase warp makes the downstroke about 22% quicker than the upstroke. Glides and dihedral banking are kept.

### Green turtle (`src/game/creatures/turtle.ts`)
- **Skin:** one baked 2048 px atlas covers the shell, head and flippers.
  - Carapace: a weighted Voronoi of 1 nuchal, 5 vertebral and 4 pairs of costal scutes, plus 12 pairs of marginals. Scutes butt edge to edge with narrow pale seams (grooves in the normal and AO maps). Each scute is olive-brown with irregular dark and amber streaks radiating from its growth centre, faint growth rings, and an algal film toward the rim.
  - Plastron: creamy yellow with seams.
  - Head: one pair of prefrontal scales, then polygonal dark scales with pale cream borders getting smaller down the sides. A dark horny beak, a mouth line, and a pale throat and neck.
  - Flippers: large pale-edged scales along the leading edge and smaller ones behind, one claw, a pale trailing margin and a pale underside.
- **Shape:** the shell is slightly deeper (0.21 m above and 0.095 m below the midline, from 0.19 and 0.085) and has a slight rim lip.
- **Timing:** each power stroke takes 1.5 s, with a downstroke about 25% quicker than the recovery. A 2.5–3.5 s glide follows with the flippers swept back along the shell, so propulsion is about a third of a roughly 4–5 s cycle (0.23 Hz). Turtles sometimes make two strokes in a row, and make shorter glides while climbing to breathe. Speed is 0.72 m/s while stroking and 0.5 m/s while gliding, close to the cited 0.6 m/s.

### Moon jelly (`src/game/creatures/jellies.ts`)
- **Geometry:** the bell margin has eight shallow lobes with notches at the rhopalia. The 36 tube tentacles are replaced by a thin skirt that the shader cuts into 110–220 hair-fine tentacles, each with its own length, with gaps at the rhopalia. Antialiased coverage fades tentacles thinner than a pixel toward the water colour. The four oral arms are now folded, U-section ribbons with ruffled edges.
- **Anatomy:** the anatomy is drawn by parallax on a plane inside the bell, reached by following the view ray in local space, so it shifts under the clear surface. It includes four ruffled horseshoe gonads open toward the centre, 16 radial canals (the 8 perradial and interradial ones branch twice), the ring canal, eight rhopalia and a faint four-lobed stomach. Pulse contraction is undone so the anatomy stays with the tissue.
- **Light:**
  - It still blends toward `WATER_COLOR` / `WATER_KD` / `WATER_EXTINCTION`, with depth writes kept.
  - Opacity follows the path length through the mesoglea: a fresnel rim, the milky margin and fine white nematocyst specks.
  - Forward Henyey-Greenstein scattering (g = 0.7) of the refracted sun, attenuated by Kd, makes jellies glow against the light. A wrapped transmission term and a blue ambient rim glow are added.
- **Timing:** the base rate stays at 0.24 Hz (confirmed by the measurements above), and each jelly now varies by ±25% (it was ±10%). The margin and tentacles lag the bell.

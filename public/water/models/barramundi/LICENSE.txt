Barramundi Fish
Source: Khronos glTF Sample Assets, Models/BarramundiFish
https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/BarramundiFish
Author: Microsoft, 2017
License: Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0)
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode

Changes: the mesh is centered, scaled to unit length, and turned so the head
points along +z. The vertex data is stored as raw little-endian arrays in
mesh.bin. The textures are resized to 512 px (256 px for occlusion,
roughness, and metalness) and stored as JPEG.
